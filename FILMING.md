# Filming checklist

Say the clip ID out loud first. Label to camera 2 s. Show all box sides before opening. Hold each item 2 s with its printed text facing the camera. Sellers: tape the box and end on a close-up. Landscape, 1080p, under 60 s.

Labels: print `gates/print/labels.pdf` (TUB2JN, TUB2JN spare for U9, TUB2JX, TUB2JU, 6DVME7, 5EECYY, KQWMTM, blank).
Clips go in `gates/clips/raw/` in filming order. Say the spoken ID in the first 3 s.
